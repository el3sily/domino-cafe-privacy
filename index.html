Domino Café – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026 · Developer: Mohamed Embaby (El3sely) · Contact: mohamed.y.embaby@gmail.com

Domino Café (package com.el3sily.domino) is a free dominoes game with offline gameplay and optional store purchases. This policy describes the Android and iOS release versions and explains how information is handled when you play or contact us.

Information handled by the game

No game account or sign-in is required to play. Store purchases use your Google Play or Apple account. The current game does not send personal information, gameplay events, advertising identifiers, or crash reports to us or to analytics or advertising providers. It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location.

InformationPurpose and handling
Gameplay state, campaign progress, daily puzzle attempts and streaksSaved locally to run the game, resume matches and calculate progress. This information is not uploaded to our servers.
Store purchase informationUsed to recognize and restore purchases and acknowledge or complete transactions with the store, as described below.
Appearance preferencesStored on your device to remember your selected theme.
Support messagesIf you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond to your request and resolve the issue.

Advertising, purchases and analytics

The Android and iOS versions integrate Google Play billing and Apple in-app purchases, respectively, for the optional, one-time Remove Ads purchase when available in the store. The store processes payment using your store account. The game does not receive your card number or payment credentials. Your store's privacy policy and terms govern its handling of payment and account information.

The game queries product information and purchase status and receives product identifiers, purchase identifiers, transaction dates, payment status and verification data from the store. It uses this information to recognize your Remove Ads entitlement and restore purchases at startup, when returning to the app, or when you choose Restore purchases. The billing integration sends purchase acknowledgements to Google Play, including the purchase token, or completes transactions with Apple.

Purchase details are processed in app memory. The game saves your Remove Ads entitlement and processed transaction identifiers in an encrypted file on your device to recover local ownership and avoid duplicate grants. Store verification payloads and receipts are not saved in this file. The encryption key is protected by device secure storage. We do not send these details to a developer-operated server or log receipts or transaction identifiers. Store processing is not restricted to your device and is governed by its own policy. When available, the store can restore purchases; local storage or key failures can prevent recovery of cached ownership. Deleting the game does not delete your store's purchase records. Manage purchase history or refund requests through the store.

The current version has no active third-party advertising, remote analytics or crash-reporting service. Buying Remove Ads currently causes no visible advertising change because ads are not active. Before enabling services that process additional information, we will update this policy to describe the providers, information, purposes and choices, and obtain consent where required.

Store privacy policies: Google and Apple.

Sharing and third parties

We do not sell your personal information. The game does not upload local gameplay data to us. Information you send by email is handled by our email service provider and may be disclosed when required by applicable law. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other information unnecessary for support.

Google Play and your device platform may independently process information under their own policies. This privacy-policy website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs when you visit it. See the Google Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement.

Retention and deletion

Gameplay progress, daily-puzzle records, in-progress matches and purchase entitlement data are saved in an encrypted file on your device. Pass-and-play player names you enter are included in saved match data. Appearance preferences are stored separately on your device. You can remove stored app data using your device settings or uninstall the game. Device backups, if any, are controlled by your operating system and account settings. We have no server copy of your local gameplay data to recover or delete.

Support emails are retained for as long as needed to address your request, maintain necessary support records, or meet legal obligations. To request deletion of support information, email us with the subject “Delete my data”.

Children

Domino Café is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can address it and delete it where applicable.

Your choices and rights

You can play without creating a game account, making a purchase or sending a support message. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of personal information, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us to make a request. We may need enough information to verify and respond to the request; do not send sensitive identity documents unless we explain why they are necessary.

Changes

We will update this page and its “Last updated” date when our practices change. Material changes will be communicated in the game where appropriate.
