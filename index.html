Domino Café – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026 · Developer: Mohamed Embaby (El3sely) · Contact: mohamed.y.embaby@gmail.com

Domino Café (package com.el3sily.domino) is a free offline dominoes game. This policy explains how the current version handles information when you play or contact us.

Information handled by the game

No account or sign-in is required. The current game does not send personal information, gameplay events, advertising identifiers, or crash reports to us or to analytics or advertising providers. It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location.

InformationPurpose and handling
Gameplay state, campaign progress, daily puzzle attempts and streaksUsed locally to run the game and calculate progress. This information is not uploaded to our servers.
Appearance preferencesStored on your device to remember your selected theme.
Support messagesIf you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond to your request and resolve the issue.

Advertising, purchases and analytics

The current version has no active third-party advertising, in-app billing, remote analytics, or crash-reporting service. Advertising and an optional Remove Ads purchase are planned for a later version. Before enabling services that process additional information, we will update this policy to identify the providers, data, purposes, retention and choices, and obtain consent where required. Planned services do not describe data collection by the current version.

Sharing and third parties

We do not sell your personal information. The game does not upload local gameplay data to us. Information you send by email is handled by our email service provider and may be disclosed when required by applicable law. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other information unnecessary for support.

Google Play and your device platform may independently process information under their own policies. This privacy-policy website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs when you visit it. See the Google Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement.

Retention and deletion

Local data is kept only on your device while it remains available to the app. You can remove stored app data using your device settings or uninstall the game. Device backups, if any, are controlled by your operating system and account settings. We have no server copy of your local gameplay data to recover or delete.

Support emails are retained for as long as needed to address your request, maintain necessary support records, or meet legal obligations. To request deletion of support information, email us with the subject “Delete my data”.

Children

Domino Café is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can address it and delete it where applicable.

Your choices and rights

You can play without creating an account or sending a support message. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of personal information, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us to make a request. We may need enough information to verify and respond to the request; do not send sensitive identity documents unless we explain why they are necessary.

Changes

We will update this page and its “Last updated” date when our practices change. Material changes will be communicated in the game where appropriate.
